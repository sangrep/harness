# Source origin

Repository-owned packaging, integration, tests, examples and documentation are
prepared for this standalone component. Fixtures are synthetic and must contain no
customer material or real provider records. Generic engine source is admitted only
from an independently sanitized, reviewed one-time source inventory. Source history
and private origin identifiers are not imported.

`provenance/contracts-v1.json` records the public contracts dependency, consumed
through a reproduced and digest-verified wheel. Contract source is not bundled into
the harness distribution.

`provenance/repository-v1.json` records technical source classifications and the
license-text digest. Technical provenance checks establish inventory consistency;
they do not establish specialist legal acceptance, copyright ownership, contributor
rights or permission to publish. The existing `LICENSE` publication gate applies.
